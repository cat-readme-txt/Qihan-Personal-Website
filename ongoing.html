01 / Computational physics

Nuclear pasta simulation

Working under Dr. Soham Pal at Illinois on CUDA-based particle simulations of dense nuclear matter. I implemented an FP64 all-pairs force kernel with periodic boundaries and shifted-force cutoffs, plus CPU/GPU validation and CUDA-event benchmarks. Development uses Illinois Computes Research Notebooks (ICRN), a browser-accessible research environment with NVIDIA GPU options.

About ICRN

02 / Computer music

DISSCO GPU acceleration

Working with Professor Sever Tipei on GPU acceleration for DISSCO, a system for composition and additive sound synthesis. My focus is the costly rendering path: generating and combining individual sound partials, with loudness processing and effects. My work is to develop a CUDA path that detects compatible local or external NVIDIA GPUs, falls back to the CPU when needed, and can run on cloud-hosted GPUs.

DISSCO source

03 / Semiconductor devices

Thin-film fabrication

Working alongside graduate researcher Peter Wilson in Professor Kevin Kim’s Thin Film and Charged Particle Research Lab. Our current area is gallium-oxide thin films for power and optoelectronic devices. I am onboarding now; my specific fabrication and photonics tasks are still being defined.

04 / Embedded systems

Illini Solar Car

On the sensors and hardware-in-the-loop teams, I am evaluating weather-resistant blind-spot sensors for Delos, targeting side and rear obstacle detection powered by the car’s existing 12 V system. I configured a preliminary Zephyr RTOS environment for planned HIL testing; final electrical-component choices will guide firmware integration.
