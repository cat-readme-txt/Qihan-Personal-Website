02 / Featured project Digital hardware · SystemVerilog · 2026

Pipelined INT8
FPGA accelerator.

Eight signed INT8 lanes.
One result per cycle without stalls.

Built a four-stage streaming dot-product engine with exact 19-bit accumulation and ready/valid backpressure. The registered reduction tree keeps each vector's arithmetic exact from signed products to the final sum.

Quantum computing · HackMidwest · 2026

ML-assisted quantum decoder

Built a hybrid decoder for the Gross [[144,12,12]] code using seeded PriorTilt candidates, Relay-BP escalation, neural candidate selection, and learned priors. The project placed second in the Infleqtion Challenge 1 track.

In saved-shot tests at p = 0.005, the frozen hierarchy reduced failed pairs from 845 to 479 per 10,000 versus Relay-BP and mean pair latency from 1,471 to 825 ms. These are local evaluation results.
