02 / Featured project Digital hardware · SystemVerilog · 2026

Pipelined INT8
FPGA accelerator.

Eight signed INT8 lanes.
One result per cycle without stalls.

Built a four-stage streaming dot-product engine with exact 19-bit accumulation and ready/valid backpressure. The registered reduction tree keeps each vector's arithmetic exact from signed products to the final sum.
