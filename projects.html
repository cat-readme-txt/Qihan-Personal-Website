Quantum computing · HackMidwest / Infleqtion

ML-assisted quantum decoder

A decoder must recover useful quantum information from a pattern of noisy checks. I built a hierarchy for the Gross [[144,12,12]] code that sends most shots through fast candidates and escalates harder ones.

Two independently seeded PriorTilt candidates, Relay-BP fallback, and a learned candidate selector work together with priors for 8,784 circuit-error mechanisms. The project placed second in the Infleqtion Challenge 1 track.
