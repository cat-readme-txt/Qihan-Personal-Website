02 / Research

Signals → understanding

Independent research · Researcher · 2026

Resource-constrained
EEG motor classification

View repository

How well can lightweight classifiers distinguish left- and right-fist movement—and imagined movement—in people they haven’t seen before?

Why it matters

Brain–computer interfaces must work within real constraints: fewer electrodes, shorter decision windows, limited calibration, and noisy signals. Accuracy is only part of the problem.

What I did

I designed and implemented the benchmark, built the EEG preprocessing pipeline, ran the statistical analyses, and prepared the visualizations and manuscript.

Technical methods

Band power, Hjorth parameters, wavelet energy, common spatial patterns (CSP), and filter-bank CSP, paired with shrinkage linear discriminant analysis. Leave-one-subject-out evaluation measures transfer to unseen participants; further experiments test calibration, channel reduction, short windows, and simulated artifacts.

Tools & languages

Python · MNE · NumPy · pandas · SciPy · scikit-learn · PyWavelets · Matplotlib · Jupyter

01 / Computational physics

Nuclear pasta simulation

Working under Dr. Soham Pal at Illinois on CUDA-based particle simulations of dense nuclear matter. I implemented an FP64 all-pairs force kernel with periodic boundaries and shifted-force cutoffs, plus CPU/GPU validation and CUDA-event benchmarks. Development uses Illinois Computes Research Notebooks (ICRN), a browser-accessible research environment with NVIDIA GPU options.

02 / Computer music

DISSCO GPU acceleration

Working with Professor Sever Tipei on GPU acceleration for DISSCO, a system for composition and additive sound synthesis. My focus is the costly rendering path: generating and combining individual sound partials, with loudness processing and effects. My work is to develop a CUDA path that detects compatible local or external NVIDIA GPUs, falls back to the CPU when needed, and can run on cloud-hosted GPUs.
